# Code of Conduct

Playdub is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and obtain consent before sharing their information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- insults, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- abusing project infrastructure or the security-reporting process; and
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed in the
repository's GitHub community profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public
issue.

Maintainers will review reports in confidence, avoid conflicts of interest, and may edit or remove
content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation.
They will consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and the safety of affected community members.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing Playdub.
